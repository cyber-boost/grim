## 1. GRIM MARKETPLACE (30% revenue share)

### Concept
User-generated modules, scripts, and integrations sold through official marketplace

### Revenue Model
- **Commission**: 30% of all sales (industry standard)
- **Listing Fees**: $29/month for premium listings
- **Certification**: $199 for "Grim Certified" badge

### Implementation
```bash
# Example marketplace integration
grim marketplace search backup-aws
grim marketplace install user/aws-backup-pro --price $49
grim marketplace publish my-custom-module --price $29
```

### Projected Revenue (Year 2)
- 500 active marketplace users
- Average $50/month in sales per user
- 30% commission = $7,500/month
- **Annual Marketplace Revenue**: $90,000
