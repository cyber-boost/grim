## Email Marketing Best Practices

### Segmentation Strategy
- **Trial Users:** Welcome series, feature education
- **Active Users:** Nurture series, advanced features
- **Inactive Users:** Re-engagement series
- **Enterprise Prospects:** Custom enterprise campaigns

### Personalization
- **Industry-specific content**
- **Company size targeting**
- **Role-based messaging**
- **Behavioral triggers**

### A/B Testing
- **Subject lines**
- **Send times**
- **Content variations**
- **CTA placement**

### Performance Metrics
- **Open rates**
- **Click-through rates**
- **Conversion rates**
- **Unsubscribe rates**
- **Revenue attribution**

### Automation Rules
- **Welcome series:** Triggered on signup
- **Nurture series:** Based on engagement
- **Re-engagement:** Based on inactivity
- **Win-back:** Based on churn risk
